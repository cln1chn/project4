cole chen ⋆⋆⋆

Cole Chen is a senior at Louisiana State University pursuing a BFA in Graphic Design and serves as president of the Graphic Design Student Association. From his experience as a first-generation Chinese American to his passion for R&B music, Chen approaches branding and interactive media through a lens of cultural nuance. his work is driven by design that feels intentional and deeply grounded in the human experience.
